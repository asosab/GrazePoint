Data, sources and licenses

Updated November 15, 2026.

What you upload

The polygons, soil tests (PRIAS), plantings, forage estimates, grazing records and herd data are yours. Field Shift only stores them and crosses them with the climate to hand the analysis back to you.

Climate

  • NASA POWER: rain, temperature and soil moisture at your coordinates, daily series and climatology. Open data from NASA.
  • NASA GIBS: tiles for NDVI (MODIS), land surface temperature (MODIS), root-zone moisture (SMAP L4), monthly rain (MERRA-2), heat spots and true color. Open imagery from NASA EOSDIS.

Soil

  • SoilGrids, from ISRIC – World Soil Information: pH, clay and organic carbon when the farm has no PRIAS on record. CC-BY 4.0 license.

Base maps

  • Esri World Imagery and Esri reference layers: used under Esri's terms.
  • OpenStreetMap (optional street map): © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL license.

The code

Field Shift is free software under the MIT license. The full repository is at github.com/asosab/fieldShift.

An honest warning

Satellite data arrives days late and at hundreds of meters per pixel. It works for comparing zones and dates inside your farm; it does not replace walking the paddock or measuring in the field.
