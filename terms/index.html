Terms and conditions

In force since November 15, 2026.

What Field Shift is

Field Shift is a free, open source tool that helps you decide what to plant and when on your farm. It crosses what you upload (polygons, soil tests, plantings, forage estimates) with NASA Earth observation and other open sources. It was born at the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 and is maintained by volunteers.

What it is not

Field Shift is not agronomic advice. The suggested rotations, planting windows and forage states come from satellite data and from what you record: they are estimates to inform your judgment, not a recipe. The satellite estimates; the technician's eye decides. Every planting, grazing or investment decision you make with this data is yours.

Your account

To use the app you create an account with an email and a password, and we verify the email with a code. Keep your password safe and tell us if someone gets in without permission. You need to be old enough to sign a contract in your country.

Your data

What you load into Field Shift stays yours. You only allow us to use it to run the service: store it, cross it with the climate and hand reports back to you. You can fix it, download your report as a PDF and delete your account from your account sheet inside the app, whenever you want. Deleting the account removes your farms, plots and records with it.

Third-party data

The app queries public services: NASA POWER and NASA GIBS, SoilGrids from ISRIC, Esri imagery and OpenStreetMap. Each source has its own terms and licenses, listed on the data page.

Acceptable use

You can use Field Shift for your farm or for the farms you advise. Do not hammer the API with automated queries, resell the service or use it for anything illegal. If you abuse it, we can suspend the account.

Availability

Field Shift is an open project maintained by volunteers, offered as is: it can fail, change or go offline without notice. Third-party services (NASA, Esri, ISRIC) can fail too.
