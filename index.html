GrazePoint

The digital PRIAS for your farm: what to plant after your last crop, and in which rain window.

Open the map

What it does

  • Rotation picks. Three ranked options from your last soil test, the previous crop and your local rainfall.
  • Forage planting window. The months when rain is enough to establish pasture, checked against your soil's current state.
  • Plot record. Polygons drawn on the satellite image, planting history, PRIAS soil tests and forage estimates in one place, instead of paper and Excel.
  • PDF report. Ready to forward to the producer on WhatsApp.

Where the data comes from

  • NASA POWER: rain, temperature and soil moisture at your coordinates, daily series and climatology.
  • Your farm: your soil tests, your plantings, your forage estimates. Without your data there is no recommendation worth trusting.

Built with Hacienda Patiño (Santa Cruz, Bolivia) for sandy soils and regenerative ranching, but it works for any farm.
